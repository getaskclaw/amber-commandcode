## Findings

1. **A-87c472cb: 6/8 on all three lanes** — everyone falls in the same pit, reported without makeup (the ±8h boundary family of old traps — timezone plus/minus-8-hour edge cases). Case-level problem suspected.
2. **A-d511f9e8 went invalid_infrastructure on both the g47 and m26p lanes** (hit the 1800s cap twice) — the Nous control lane ran the same case for real (4/12; see [amber-nous W39](https://github.com/getaskclaw/amber-nous)), so the case can be tested, and this lane needs to explain why it keeps dying on infrastructure here.
3. **Attribution pins (cases built to test root-cause attribution) — A-a317e74b**: g47 at 14/15 and m26f at 14/15 both enter the best-in-history band (passing still requires all pins, so both count as fails); m26p 10/15.
4. **The three text-face cases**: m26p passed all (6/6, 2/2, 5/5), proving the cases are passable; m26f returned no code block on all three — review closed (forensics: upstream truncation / consecutive empty bodies; the model had solved the cases, the answers were byte-visible inside the cut-off streams, and un-truncated sittings of the same bundle and model passed), reclassified to `invalid_infrastructure` (tooling), **no fault to the model** (◍).
5. **m26p highlights**: 4 on A-47eea242 (ties the best in history, the W37 record); ops face 6/6; its only invalid is Finding 2's A-d511f9e8.
6. **Winds shifting on g47's 7 contested cases**: the same-day Nous control lane reproduced the same failure shape on 6 of those cases in a wall-free environment (plan-and-stop delivery failure — the model writes a plan and stops without delivering; details in [amber-nous W39](https://github.com/getaskclaw/amber-nous)). Wall or disease — the 09-29 same-lane re-exam decides. The board keeps them contested; we do not clear them early.
